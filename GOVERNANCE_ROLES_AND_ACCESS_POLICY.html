MANDATORY POLICY NOTICE: This charter establishes the formal digital permissions, administrative boundaries, and operational safeguards governing the OneFellowship mobile application. Every authorized official and covenant member is strictly bound by these standards upon ratification.

1. Executive Summary & Governance Principles

The OneFellowship Welfare mobile platform is structured on an immutable foundation of Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), cryptographic logging, and multi-tier biometric security. The system architecture enforces four core tenets:

  • Separation of Powers: Financial custody, registry administration, and executive discipline are kept distinct. No single individual possesses unchecked power.
  • Triple Leadership Authorization (Clause 5): Every financial disbursement requires three (3) independent executive signatures before funds can be released.
  • Confidential Audit Trails (Clause 6): System activity logs are restricted exclusively to Administrative Officials and the Chairperson to prevent tampering and protect member privacy.
  • Direct Member Voice: Every registered member has an encrypted, confidential channel to submit concerns, complaints, and welfare needs directly to the Executive Leadership.

2. Defined Roles & Scope of Authority

Assembly Member
1. General Member
Ratified covenant member of the fellowship.
Key Responsibilities: Maintain active status through monthly welfare contributions (KES 1,000); keep verified KYC, Next of Kin, Dependants, and Residential Location updated. System Clearance: Personal Dashboard, Virtual ID Card, Payment Ledger & Receipts, Constitution Charter, and Direct Feedback Submission.
Financial Custodian
2. Treasurer
Chief custodian of funds, ledger, and disbursements.
Key Responsibilities: Monitor collections and bank accounts; reconcile payment entries; record expenses with mandatory receipts; serve as First Signatory for payouts. System Clearance: Organization Wallet, Cash Balance Metrics, Expense Recording, Co-signing Disbursements (Clause 5), and Financial Data Exports.
Operations & Registry
3. Administrator / Secretary
Registrar, communications director, and records manager.
Key Responsibilities: Maintain member registry; verify member KYC identity; dispatch broadcast alerts; receive and resolve member concerns; review audit trails. System Clearance: Member Registry Management, Push Broadcasts, Feedback Review & Response, Security & Audit Logs, and Co-signing Disbursements.
Supreme Oversight
4. Chairperson
Executive Chairman of the Board of Trustees.
Key Responsibilities: Preside over fellowship meetings and disciplinary tribunals; execute member terminations under Article 7; approve role promotions; grant final fund approvals. System Clearance: Full Executive Clearance (All system modules), Member Termination & Reinstatement, Role Assignment, and Final Fund Disbursement Sign-off.

4. Special Fiduciary & Operational Safeguards

🛡️ Triple Leadership Authorization for Expenses (Clause 5)

No single official has unilateral disbursement authority. All expenses entered into the system must achieve three (3) independent signatures (e.g., Treasurer, Administrator, Chairperson). Automated push notifications alert signers upon expense entry and progress milestones. An official cannot sign twice for the same transaction.

🔒 Audit Trail Confidentiality & Integrity (Clause 6)

Every system action—from payment approvals and role modifications to broadcast messages and login attempts—is permanently written to a tamper-proof audit log. Access is strictly quarantined to Administrators and the Chairperson to prevent intimidation or financial interference.

⛔ Article 7 Account Lockout Safeguards

Members terminated for constitutional default (Two-Case Default rule) or gross misconduct are immediately barred from the app. Upon launch, a formal revocation notice confirms grounds of expulsion, date of action, Next of Kin formal service, and authorized appeal procedures.
