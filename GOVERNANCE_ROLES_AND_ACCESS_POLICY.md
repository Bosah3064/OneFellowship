## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

The OneFellowship Welfare Mobile System utilizes strict **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** paired with **cryptographic and biometric guards** to protect welfare contributions, enforce fiduciary discipline, and guarantee absolute transparency.

To prevent fraud, embezzlement, or unilateral authority over collective funds:
1. **Separation of Powers:** No single official possesses unilateral authority over finances, member terminations, or governance logs.
2. **Triple Leadership Authorization (Clause 5):** All fund withdrawals and expense disbursements strictly require **three (3) independent executive signatures** before funds can be released.
3. **Audit Trail Exclusivity:** Security and financial audit logs are restricted exclusively to Administrative Officials and the Chairperson to prevent tampering or intimidation.
4. **Confidential Feedback:** Members are provided a direct, encrypted channel to report concerns, complaints, and welfare needs directly to the Executive Leadership.

## 2. Defined Roles & Institutional Responsibilities

### 1. General Member (`member`)
* **Constitutional Status:** Ratified covenant member of the welfare fellowship.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  - Maintain active standing through regular welfare contributions (KES 1,000 per case / monthly).
  - Submit and maintain verified KYC information (National ID, Next of Kin, Dependants, Residential County/Country).
  - Abide by the ratifying covenants of the Welfare Constitution.
* **Granted System Privileges:**
  - Access personal member dashboard and dynamic digital ID / Virtual Membership Card.
  - View personal contribution history, receipts, and outstanding welfare balances.
  - Review the complete 9-Chapter Welfare Constitution at any time.
  - Submit confidential feedback, concerns, complaints, and welfare assistance requests.
  - Update personal credentials and configure local biometric authentication.

---

### 2. Treasurer (`treasury`)
* **Constitutional Status:** Principal Custodian of the Welfare Fund and Financial Ledger.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  - Monitor bank, MPESA paybill, and mobile collection accounts.
  - Reconcile incoming monthly contributions and registration fees.
  - Record operational, administrative, and bereavement expenses with mandatory receipt citations.
  - Serve as **First Signatory** for welfare disbursements.
* **Granted System Privileges:**
  - Full access to the **Organization Wallet** and real-time cash balance metrics.
  - Ability to record expenses and disburse payout allocations.
  - Co-sign and authorize pending expense disbursements (Triple Authorization Workflow).
  - Access to payment reconciliation and financial transaction ledgers.
  - Export financial spreadsheets and ledgers for annual church audits.
* **Restricted / Denied Areas:**
  - ❌ Cannot view or alter Administrative Security Audit Trails.
  - ❌ Cannot terminate members or alter membership disciplinary status.
  - ❌ Cannot change member system roles.

---

### 3. Administrator / Executive Secretary (`admin`)
* **Constitutional Status:** Chief Administrative Officer, Registrar, and Custodian of Records.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  - Maintain the official member registry and verify KYC identification.
  - Dispatch official broadcast communications, alerts, and meeting agendas.
  - Receive, investigate, and officially resolve member feedback and confidential concerns.
  - Monitor security logs, failed access attempts, and audit trails.
* **Granted System Privileges:**
  - Full access to **Member Management**: approve registrations, inspect full KYC sheets, and export member directories.
  - Access to **Broadcast Notifications**: send high-priority push alerts to all members or target cohorts.
  - Access to **Member Feedback & Concerns**: review open submissions, update resolution status, and send direct responses.
  - Full access to **Security & Audit Logs**: inspect chronological trails of all system modifications, logins, and authorizations.
  - Co-sign and authorize pending expense disbursements (Triple Authorization Workflow).
* **Restricted / Denied Areas:**
  - ❌ Cannot unilaterally terminate members without Chairperson assent.
  - ❌ Cannot disburse organization funds independently without Treasurer and Chairperson signatures.

---

### 4. Chairperson (`chairperson`)
* **Constitutional Status:** Supreme Executive Leader and Chairman of the Board of Trustees.
* **Core Responsibilities:**
  - Preside over executive committee meetings, annual general assemblies, and disciplinary tribunals.
  - Exercise constitutional disciplinary authority under Article 7 (Member Terminations & Reinstatements).
  - Approve changes to official member roles (elevations to Treasurer or Secretary).
  - Authorize emergency disbursements and sign all final fund approvals.
* **Granted System Privileges:**
  - **Full Executive Clearance:** Holds all system permissions across Governance, Finance, and Security.
  - Exclusive authority to execute **Member Termination** (Article 7 Disciplinary Safeguards).
  - Authority to **Reinstate Terminated Members** following formal appeals.
  - Authority to **Promote or Modify Member Roles** (`member`, `treasury`, `admin`, `chairperson`).
  - Final signing authority for Organization Wallet disbursements.

## 4. Special Fiduciary & Operational Safeguards

### A. Triple Leadership Authorization for Expenses (Clause 5)
Under Clause 5 of the OneFellowship Constitution, **no single individual may authorize a financial payout**.
- Every disbursement request entered into the system requires **three (3) independent executive signatures** before status is marked `APPROVED`.
- The signing interface requires explicit confirmation of expenditure legitimacy and acknowledgment of criminal liability under Anti-Fraud Policy Clause 7.
- The system automatically detects duplicate authorizations and bars an official from signing more than once on the same transaction.
- When an expense is recorded or needs signing, automated broadcast notifications are dispatched to all eligible leaders.

### B. Audit Trail Integrity & Exclusivity (Clause 6)
- Every system transaction, role change, broadcast notification, and login attempt is written to an append-only cryptographic log.
- Audit trails are strictly restricted to `admin` and `chairperson`. The Treasurer and regular members cannot view or manipulate logs, ensuring impartial oversight.

### C. Member Termination Protocol (Article 7)
- Termination immediately revokes app access, invalidates credentials, and terminates active tokens.
- Upon launch, terminated accounts are blocked by the `TerminatedAccountScreen`, which presents:
  - Formal Grounds of Revocation (e.g., Two-Case Default, Embezzlement, Misconduct).
  - Enforced Date and Article 7 Notice confirming formal notification has been served to the Next of Kin.
  - Sign-out button and official Welfare Executive appeal channel.
