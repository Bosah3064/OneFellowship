Privacy Policy for OneFellowship

Together We Stand • Effective Date: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

OneFellowship ("we", "our", or "us") operates the OneFellowship mobile application and member management portal. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

While using our application, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, phone number, physical address.
  • Membership Details: Member ID, membership status, registration date, emergency contact details.
  • Financial & Contribution Records: Records of welfare dues, monthly fellowship contributions, and transaction receipts.
  • Photos: Profile photos or membership card photos uploaded by you for your virtual ID card.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Provide, maintain, and administer membership within OneFellowship.
  • Generate your digital virtual membership card.
  • Maintain transparent ledger records for welfare contributions and community savings.
  • Send important notifications regarding membership status, events, and dues.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored and authenticated using Google Firebase enterprise-grade infrastructure. All transmissions between your mobile device and our backend servers are strictly encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party advertisers or external commercial parties. Data is accessible solely to authorized fellowship administrators for community record-keeping.

6. Account Deletion and Data Requests

You have the right to request access to or deletion of your personal account data at any time. To request deletion, please contact your fellowship administrator or email us at Lame3064@gmail.com.
